## Current code and integration boundaries

1. **Gate and invocation.** `app/Services/Entrepreneurs/ReleaseGate/ReleaseGate.php` obtains current source, policy and risk decisions and calls `PhaseOneRules::evaluator()`. `SourceCapture` records plan sections, budget revision/input/output JSON, claim versions, evidence identities and retained required horizon. It hashes entrepreneur service dependencies. `AssessmentGate`, `PlanBudgetCoherence`, `PlanBudgetGuide`, `PlanIssueReadiness` and `FounderBriefRelease` adapt the existing workflow to that gate. Twenty rules are enabled; seventeen remain deferred. Extend this evaluator and its adapters rather than introduce another release verdict. The assessment-history correction retains current checks on the latest assessment without repeating them for every historical row.

2. **Stored inputs and outputs.** `BusinessPlan` and `PlanSection` hold answers, metadata, attachment references and completion state. `EntrepreneurBudget` stores revision, assumptions, launch/fixed/future costs, revenue, funding and computed JSON. `EntrepreneurBudgetService::update()` uses a locked transaction, revision check, audit event and the existing calculator. `Portal/EntrepreneurPlanBudgetController` validates the founder's update; advisor correction and assessment-recalculation controllers also call the service. Their current contracts cannot silently accept or erase a new model version. The existing monthly editors and validation stop at twelve explicit values; `BudgetMonthlySchedule` fills missing values using growth, while debtor/creditor days become whole-month lags in `BudgetCalculator`. These are legacy inputs, not a complete Phase 2 model. A separate versioned driver input/output contract must preserve missing values and protect existing budgets until an explicit reconstruction is saved. `BusinessPlanSnapshot::captureInputs()` must capture the selected engine/version without reintroducing derived release evaluation into input fingerprints.

3. **PDF generation.** `BusinessPlanPreviewRenderer`, `FunderReadyBusinessPlanBuilder`, `BudgetPackBuilder` and `FunderReadyBriefBuilder` assemble the working plan, full plan, pack and brief. Primary rendering uses `PdfRenderer`/Browsershot; structured text fallbacks remain. Phase 1 now routes these through `DraftDelivery` and `BudgetDraft`, including source rechecks and separate primary/fallback provenance. Phase 2 changes the financial payload and labels; it does not certify regenerated PDFs or implement the retained-artifact programme early.

4. **AI narrative.** `BusinessPlanExecutiveSummary` uses `entrepreneur.plan_executive_summary`; `Guidance` uses `entrepreneur.plan_guidance` and `entrepreneur.plan_requirement_assist`; `Assessment` uses `entrepreneur.plan_score_criterion`; `EntrepreneurPromptRegistry` also owns idea-validation prompts. They consume saved plan/budget data through `AiClient`. New quantitative outputs must originate in the deterministic engine. Typed narrative-token binding and answer supersession remain Phase 3. No AI inference supplies missing cash dates, tax eligibility, capacity or F2 decisions.

5. **Xero and actuals.** `LiveXeroClient::financialSnapshot()` fetches ProfitAndLoss, BalanceSheet and BankSummary for one selected period. Its period search checks the previous 1-36 months and selects the first with activity, or the preceding-month fallback. `FinancialSnapshotPuller` records immutable client/accounting-connection snapshots with period, provider, badge and degraded state. These contain aggregate P&L, balance and bank figures, not every elapsed month's classified receipts/payments and obligations. The field named operating cash flow comes from BankSummary net movement. `FallbackXeroClient` can substitute fake data when integration is disabled; the puller can default missing dates to now. Neither is acceptable actuals proof. Phase 2 therefore needs reviewed monthly actuals with source-period/import-version/account mappings, closing bank/restricted cash and AR/AP reconciliation, plus documented manual entry. Verify the entrepreneur-to-client connection before importing. Missing records are MI-07 Cannot verify; explicit evidenced pre-trading is a separate case.

6. **Implementation locations.** Add cohesive pure services under `app/Services/Entrepreneurs/Forecast/` for the versioned driver model, calendar, shared capacity, expenses/owner pay, obligations and dated cash ledger, actuals handover, scenarios, tax/depreciation, metrics and funding sizing. Use concrete immutable contracts and decimal arithmetic with a recorded rounding policy. Keep persistence and authorisation in the existing budget workflow, extracting focused request/storage adapters to respect size limits. Add a version-aware `ReleaseGate` facts adapter and Phase 2 rule implementations; preserve the legacy `BudgetFacts` interpretation for F1. Current `PlanClaimVersion`, `PlanGateRun`, `PlanRuleConfigVersion` and `PlanRuleDecision` stay authoritative. Full typed narrative facts, `PlanReleaseSnapshot`/`PlanReleaseArtifact` and private retained bytes remain Phase 3; claim-evidence classification remains Phase 4. Register the new engine paths at the existing 90% critical-coverage threshold and disclose formula owners through the methodology registry.

7. **Routes and fallback contract.** Rechecked advisor and portal document controllers and brief builder against the route inventory in [the Phase 1 mapping](release-gate-phase-1-mapping.md#4-documents-routes-and-fallbacks). That complete inventory remains the integration checklist: latest/selected plan previews; latest/selected Budget Pack PDFs; funder-ready full plan and brief; founder-brief preview; both assessment snapshot previews; portal plan preview and pack HTML/PDF; release approval, reconciliation and summary retry. Inline and downloaded variants, primary and fallback bytes must consume the same selected model/source and Draft contract. Evidence downloads retain their existing ownership/scan checks. No new share route is proposed. External release remains unavailable until later-phase prerequisites are implemented.

8. **Exact replacements.** For explicitly reconstructed version-2 budgets, replace growth-filled schedules and rounded month lags with confirmed dated drivers. Replace static reserve-minus-available arithmetic with dated unrestricted cash checkpoints, separate deficit and reserve shortfall, and a separately decided external request. Keep legacy values as historical observations, including F1's $232.73. `BudgetFundingReadiness`, `ExternalIssueReview` and gate facts must consume one result, not competing arithmetic. Preserve MI-02 provenance/timing, MI-06 retained horizon, current-input invalidation, eligible-risk limits and all arithmetic protections. The blanket attachment predicate is not removed in this phase; its replacement requires Phase 4's claim-level evidence controls. No legacy approval can activate external release. No automatic conversion of stored budgets is proposed.

9. **F1 and F2.** Keep `tests/Fixtures/Entrepreneurs/ReleaseGate/f1.json` and `f1-manifest.json` unchanged unless the Principal Advisor reviews an explicit expectation correction. F1 remains the original observation set and Draft; missing receipt/payment dates, tax/GST treatment, debt/distributions, actuals, duration/unit conversion and shared capacity remain unknown. F2 is a different reconstruction requiring agreed current offers/base membership, start/ramp, founder and provider capacity, owner-pay stages/triggers, dated unrestricted opening cash and funding, committed/deferred costs, profit allocation and intended request. Advisor decisions cover cash/source reconciliation, elapsed actuals, tax/accounting/payment policies, scope, metric definitions and evidence/risk review. Defaults are proposed policies, not consent to invent client inputs. Build and test with synthetic records while these decisions remain outstanding.

## Verification and exit

The first slice's proposed synthetic acceptance example has 80 founder hours in a month, including 16 administration and 8 business-development hours. Stream A allocates 32 hours at 8 hours per delivered unit; stream B allocates 24 hours at 8 hours per unit. Founder capacity is therefore four A units and three B units, not seven units for each stream. With one separately costed provider unit available for each stream, requested volumes of five A units at $1,000 and four B units at $600 yield $7,400 revenue and $500 contractor expense ($300 plus $200). A separately excluded $2,000 offer contributes nothing to that base case. Overallocating founder hours must report the conflict, not silently choose a winning stream. Missing hours or unit conversions must prevent a verified capacity result; an explicitly zero capacity must produce zero deliverable volume. These are proposed independent test expectations, not observations about Tania or completed test results.

Use independently calculated fixture expectations, not snapshots of the implementation. Tests cover missing versus zero, calendar boundaries/leap years, decimal precision, zero/negative contribution, shared-resource overcommitment, deposits and beyond-horizon AR/AP, owner-pay downward responses, tax losses without invented refunds, actuals immutability, late/duplicate/restricted funding, scenario input effectiveness and full cash/profit bridges. F1 must retain its original observations and unknowns; F2 is not a synthetic substitute for approved current inputs.

Run focused unit tests per calculation slice; PostgreSQL feature tests for persistence/revision/audit/permissions; regression tests for current review continuity and both direct/in-app entrepreneur navigation; static, methodology and structural guards; then full CI and browser checks for integrated changes. Keep repository coverage floors at 85% overall, 80% per module and 90% critical. Inspect representative primary/fallback PDFs when financial rendering changes.

Phase 2 is complete only when the integrated versioned engine, all required scenarios, metrics, actuals handover and nine new rule adapters meet the specification. A foundation PR or green CI alone does not establish that outcome. F2 reconstruction cannot be completed without its agreed inputs, and Release-certified remains a later programme milestone.

## Slice 2A implementation record

`Forecast/RevenueProjection` is a standalone, versioned revenue projection with explicit calendar months and immutable stream/month/founder input contracts. Decimal arithmetic caps delivered units by allocated founder hours plus costed provider capacity. Both preparation and delivery consume time; administration and business development consume the same shared founder budget. Base totals exclude upside offers, and unknown membership prevents a known base total. Upside rows are individual potential projections, not proof that they can run simultaneously with the base; including one requires a fresh shared-capacity calculation.

Missing months, prices, capacity, units, evidence status, bookings and invoicing remain visible. Known delivery arithmetic can remain visible when a separate booking/invoice input is missing, but the monthly result is Cannot verify. Explicit zero capacity produces zero delivery. Overallocated founder time and zero hours-per-unit produce Fail, retain any separate missing inputs, and suppress unusable base totals. Negative/malformed decimal inputs, duplicate IDs and out-of-calendar schedules are rejected instead of coerced or silently discarded. The monetary policy rounds capacity down to eight decimal places, rounds each stream-month monetary line half up to cents, then sums those lines. Input hashes are insensitive to stream/map ordering and change with actual input/evidence changes.

This slice enables **no additional gate rules**. It supplies tested calculation inputs for future PM-05/06/11 adapters. Existing stored budgets, save/recalculate routes, forecasts and F1 are unchanged; the new engine is not selectable through the application yet. This is not a complete cash model, an evidence-verification decision or F2. The existing broad entrepreneur-service dependency hash includes new service files, so deploying this foundation can invalidate recorded release-risk dependencies even though it does not recalculate client budgets.

The synthetic `tests/Fixtures/Entrepreneurs/Forecast/revenue-drivers.json` independently expects $7,400 base revenue, $500 contractor expense and 80 allocated founder hours, with a separate excluded $2,000 potential line. It is not a substitute for approved F2 inputs. Critical coverage lists every new engine/contract file at the unchanged 90% threshold. Methodology registration describes the formula and its not-yet-activated status.

Local verification on 6 November 2026 passed all 55 forecast, methodology and F1 tests (1,406 assertions). The seven new forecast files have 139/139 covered statements in the focused Clover report. PHPStan passed without baseline changes; the structural, boundary-contract and mass-assignment guards passed. F1's fixture and manifest are unchanged, with twenty enabled rules and seventeen deferred. Exact-commit CI evidence is recorded on the slice's pull request; local coverage does not substitute for the full-suite coverage gates.
